Learning from
work already underway.

Listening and civic bridge-building have a substantial history. Kin Vow is an untested proposal. These links offer context, not endorsement or a claim that our approach works.

A 2024 study found that several brief interventions, including humanizing people across political differences, reduced partisan animosity. A 2025 review and experiments found that many such effects were modest and faded quickly. We should test useful behavior and its limits directly.

  • Science: testing 25 approaches to partisan animosity and antidemocratic attitudes
  • PNAS: why lasting depolarization is difficult

  • The Listen First pledge
