Get Involved

There is a place for you here.

You do not need to be a student, a teacher, or an expert. You just need to believe that every person is worth hearing.

Ways to Participate

Pick what fits you.

Start a chapter

Bring Kin Vow to your school. We will help you with the constitution, the framework, and the community. You bring the people.

Learn how →

Spread the word

Share the Kin Vow message with your network. Talk about the universal language barrier. Challenge people to think about the gap in their own lives. Every conversation plants a seed.

Share your story

Have you experienced the gap between what you know and what you can express? Your story matters. It helps others see themselves in this movement.

Advise a chapter

If you are a teacher, counselor, or school staff member, consider becoming a faculty advisor for a Kin Vow chapter. Your support makes the space possible.

Contribute resources

Research, activities, discussion guides, creative exercises. If you have something that could help chapters hold better space, we want to hear about it.

Stay connected

Not ready to commit yet? No problem. Sign up below and we will keep you in the loop as the movement grows.

Say Hello

We would love to hear from you.

Whether you have questions, ideas, resources to share, or just want to connect, drop us a line.
